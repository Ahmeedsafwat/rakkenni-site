Park now · اركن دلوقتي

Street zones, no booking needed

Every street zone has a sign with a short code. Park, type the code, and you are covered for as long as you pay.

  1. Type the zone code from the sign, like 14B
  2. Pick your car and time, in 30-minute steps
  3. Pay in the app, or in cash to the registered attendant
  4. Get a reminder before time runs out and extend remotely

Outside paid hours, street parking is free.

For cities and parking operators

Every zone, every payment, in one dashboard

Rakkenni gives authorities and licensed operators a live view of street parking under Law 150 of 2020. Zones run on signs, phones and a web dashboard, with no meters or cameras to install.

  • Revenue today and this month, by zone and by district
  • App payments vs cash collected by attendants
  • Active sessions and estimated occupancy
  • Checks and cash collected by each attendant

Demo zones in the app today: Nasr City (Abbas El Akkad, Makram Ebeid, Mostafa El Nahas, Youssef Abbas) and Heliopolis (Korba, El Merghany, Roxy, El Hegaz).
